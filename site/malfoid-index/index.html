About the index
What is Malfoid?

Malfoid is a fan nickname for drawings that reimagine Draco Malfoy as a woman. Female Draco art existed before the name, but a wave of posts in September 2026 made the version widely recognizable. The character is fan-created and not part of official Harry Potter canon. The art led to memes, fanfiction and a fan-made game.

Key dates: March 2024, the earliest female Draco art on X. 22 September 2026, a viral drawing of Malfoid teasing Harry. Late September 2026, remixes and new expressions spread the format. Full timeline on malfoid.com →
